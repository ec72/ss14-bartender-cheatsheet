# SS14 Bartender's Nested Cheatsheet (for Starlight Alpha)

A simple drink recipe helper put together for bartending on **Space Station 14** (specifically configured with **Starlight Alpha's** recipes and drink names).

If you ever tended the bar as a newbie bartender during a busy shift on Starlight, you know the pain of nested recipes like having to make Irish cream to Irish car bomb just to finish a B-52. Trying to search every other recipe in the game guidebook while people are yelling orders at the counter makes it way too easy to lose your place or forget what the original drink even was. 

I just wanted a page where I could unfold subrecipes with the main drink without going back and forth.

## What it does

* **Expandable Sub-Recipes:** Click **`+ Sub-Recipe`** on any mixed ingredient to see how it's made right there on the same screen.

* **Order Pinboard:** Pin active orders at the top so you can keep up with all the orders.

* **Local:** Everything is packed into a single `index.html` file. You can just download it and double-click `index.html` on your PC to get going.

## Note

This is a small thing built with the help of AI with some manual tweaks to get the Starlight recipes and layout feeling right. It is mostly for new bartenders trying to get the recipies right in all that chaos (like me \[better start memorizing\]).

Drink data and descriptions come from the open-source **Space Station 14** and **Starlight** repos.
